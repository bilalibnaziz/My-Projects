On-device cutout models bundled with Skill Photo (run locally with onnxruntime-web; nothing is uploaded).

u2netp.onnx — U²-Net (small), general salient-object segmentation.
  Xuebin Qin et al., "U²-Net: Going Deeper with Nested U-Structure for Salient Object Detection", 2020.
  https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net — Apache License 2.0.
  ONNX export from https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg (MIT).

modnet.onnx — MODNet (8-bit quantized), portrait matting.
  Zhanghan Ke et al., "MODNet: Real-Time Trimap-Free Portrait Matting via Objective Decomposition", AAAI 2022.
  https://github.com/ZHKKKe/MODNet — Apache License 2.0.
  ONNX export from https://huggingface.co/Xenova/modnet (Apache-2.0).
